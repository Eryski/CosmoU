Welcome to CosmoU

Let’s set up your Wii U library.

Step 1

Select Cemu

CosmoU starts your games with Cemu, the Wii U emulator. Get the latest version automatically, or choose your own Cemu.exe.

Step 2

Select your Wii U games folder

The folder with your game folders or .wua, .wud and .wux files. CosmoU finds the games inside by itself.

Step 3 · Optional

Choose your menu music

Background music while you browse your games. It pauses when a game starts.

Step 4 · Optional

Pick your look

Every theme works with Covers, Grid and Wii U Look. There are 21 in Settings → Appearance.

You’re ready!
